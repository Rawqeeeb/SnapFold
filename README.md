# SnapFold — Interactive 3D Protein Folding & Sonification Game

Created by **[Roqeeb Adedeji (Roqeeb A.)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/roqeeb-adedeji/)** · **[GitHub (@Rawqeeeb)](https://github.com/Rawqeeeb/)** · **[Repository](https://github.com/Rawqeeeb/SnapFold)**

## Overview
**SnapFold** is an interactive 3D protein puzzle game where players move, turn, and spin 3D protein ribbon subunits into their native quaternary assembly using real-time psychoacoustic audio feedback.

Designed as an interactive game extension of **[SonoFold](https://sono-fold.vercel.app)** ([GitHub](https://github.com/OluwasegunIsaac/SonoFold) by Daramola Oluwasegun Isaac), **SnapFold** helps players discover and understand **protein sonification**—experiencing how amino acid sequences and 3D molecular structures translate into music.

## Key Features
- **10 Campaign Protein Levels**: Progress from 2-subunit Easy puzzles (*Human Insulin*, *Trp-Cage*, *HIV-1 Protease*, *Villin Headpiece*, *Ubiquitin*) to 3- and 4-subunit complexes (*GFP*, *Collagen Triple Helix*, *p53 Tetramer*, *Human Hemoglobin*, *KcsA Potassium Channel*).
- **Human-Friendly 3D Controls & Auto-Fit Viewport**: Move (`Left`, `Right`, `Up`, `Down`, `Closer`, `Further`) and Turn/Spin (`Tilt Up/Down`, `Turn Left/Right`, `Spin Left/Right`) with a dedicated right-hand control sidebar on desktop and a thumb-friendly bottom dock on mobile.
- **85% Snap Gate & Clear Subunit Progress**: Each subunit displays a live alignment progress bar and unlocks `🧲 Snap` once you reach `>= 85%` alignment.
- **Live 3D Protein Symphony & Video Download**: Solving a protein immediately plays its musical score across the 3D ribbon structure, lighting up each residue note-by-note. Players can copy/save their Wordle-style emoji scorecard or download a `.webm`/`.mp4` video of the 3D symphony.
- **Fold Your Own Protein (Post-Level 5)**: Unlock custom protein folding after clearing Level 5 to turn any FASTA sequence or UniProt ID into a playable 3D ribbon puzzle.
